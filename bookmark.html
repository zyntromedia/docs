Platform-Specific Tips

Windows/Ubuntu (PC)

  • Use browser sync or third-party tools like Raindrop.io or Floccus for cross-platform sync.
  • For local management, try Shaarli (self-hosted) or Bookmark Archiver (for saving web pages).

Android/iOS

  • Use Pocket or Raindrop.io for mobile-friendly bookmarking.
  • Firefox and Chrome apps support sync with desktop.

Self-Hosted Options

  • Shaarli: Self-hosted, open-source bookmark manager.
  • LinkAce: Self-hosted, supports tagging and archiving.
  • Bookmark Archiver: Save and archive bookmarks locally.

How to Choose?

  • For simplicity: Use your browser’s built-in sync.
  • For features: Try Raindrop.io or Pocket.
  • For privacy: Use Floccus or self-hosted options.
